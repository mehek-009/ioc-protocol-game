YOUR MANDATE: THE 60-MINUTE EXECUTIVE REPORT

You have 60 minutes before the executive meeting where you must present your findings. The leadership team needs clear answers to five critical questions:

  1. HOW did the attackers get in? (The Initial Access Vector)
  2. WHAT did they do? (The actions taken post-breach)
  3. WHEN did it happen? (The precise attack timeline)
  4. WHERE did they go? (Any attempted lateral movement or Command-and-Control communication)
  5. WHY weren't we protected? (Security gaps exploited)

You must identify all 12 IoCs (Indicators of Compromise) hidden across 6 investigation areas by following the established attack timeline:

Downloads → Processes → Authentication → Network → Registry → Filesystem

Every correct identification brings you closer to exposing the complete attack chain. But remember, the clock is ticking: Time is critical—the longer this investigation takes, the longer the business impact lasts.
